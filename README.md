An entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-the-word-you-d-delete-from-the-dictionary-9e774bc2): name the one word to erase from the dictionary forever.

## The word

**bologna**

It is spelled "bo-log-na" and pronounced "baloney". Delete it from the
dictionary and nothing changes: everyone already says it as if it were
spelled "baloney". The lunch meat and the slang survive; only the spelling
fraud goes. It also stays clear of the obvious picks everyone reaches for
first (moist, literally, queue, colonel): the joke here is not that the word
is ugly or hard to spell, it is that the spelling and the sound do not even
pretend to match.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the fixed word `bologna` to `/output/entry.txt` (7
characters, inside the 1-77 gate). No input, no randomness, no network.
